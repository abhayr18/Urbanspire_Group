Engineering The Impossible — Earth, Ocean & Skyline

UrbanSpire Group is an infrastructure conglomerate uniting heavy civil construction, sea-level marine pontoon engineering, and international EPC project mobilization across India and the United Arab Emirates.

Our Story & Heritage

Decades of Structural Integrity & Public Trust

Founded on the conviction that infrastructure must outlast generations, UrbanSpire Group has grown from pioneering regional civil projects in Maharashtra into a multi-disciplinary conglomerate spanning three high-performance entities.

Through UrbanSpire Construction Pvt Ltd (Pune), we execute monumental Class-I government PWD works, high-rise architectural towers, and monolithic dry-stone temples. With UrbanSpire Marine Pvt Ltd (Mumbai), we engineer heavy concrete pontoons with unsinkable EPS cores designed for 50-year marine endurance. And with UrbanSpire Global Services (Dubai), we bridge cross-border procurement and heavy machinery logistics across the GCC.
