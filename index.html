Welcome

Defining excellence for 50 years

Engineering Contracting Company (ECC) is an independent construction company, pioneering how construction is done in the UAE, and has been instrumental in sculpting the skyline of the United Arab Emirates over the past five decades.

Established in 1975, ECC is one of the oldest contracting companies in the UAE and has spent the past half-century reshaping perspectives on what’s possible. Cultivating a reputation built on trust, delivery and a tireless dedication to innovation.

Our commitment is not just to progression but to a more sustainable and meaningful world.
